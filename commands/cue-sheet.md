Cue: `$1`

Call `cue_sheet` with the title or the `track_id`. It returns what a broadcaster, platform or PRO will ask for: writers, splits, ISRC and publisher.

Pair it with `get_track` when they also need the version and stem list — which mix actually went into the edit matters as much as who wrote it.

Present it as a clean table they can paste into a delivery document. Do not fill in a missing field from memory or from the web: if the tool did not return it, say it is not in the cue sheet and point them at **info@ainsof.io**.

Every cue in the AINSOF catalogue is signed, owned and cleared — one owner, no chain-of-title archaeology. Licensing itself runs through [ainsof.io](https://ainsof.io), outside this conversation.
